# Roadmap after v0.1.0-alpha

Deferred work, intentionally outside the minimal Alpha:

- unified device lease broker across PC, Douyin, and phone backends;
- cross-project orchestration and policy routing;
- release signing, automatic updates, and a full SBOM pipeline;
- broader permission architecture for dangerous tools;
- resilient migration for private pre-Alpha runtime state;
- multi-platform and external-integration CI coverage;
- additional structured adapters plus explicit Computer Use fallback contracts;
- automated DOM compatibility monitoring and owner-guided live acceptance.

These are not implied to exist in the Alpha.
